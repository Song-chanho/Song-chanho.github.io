About

I am an integrated M.S.-Ph.D. student in the Department of Computer Science and Engineering at POSTECH. My research is in theoretical computer science, mainly focusing on algorithms for matching problems and geometric optimization. More broadly, I am interested in approximation algorithms, graph algorithms, and geometric algorithms.
